In this chapter, you'll publish the `Hello World` site, making it available outside Content Studio.

== Publishing

In Content Studio, you work in the `draft` branch. Publishing merges the selected items from `draft` to the `master` branch, which is the one visitors see.

=== Task: Publish the site

. *Select the site* - Select the `Hello World` site in the content grid.

. *Open the Publishing Wizard* - Select btn:[Publish Tree] in the action menu in the top right corner. The Publishing Wizard opens.

. *Mark the content as ready* - Items that are `In progress` block publishing. Click btn:[Mark as ready] to fix this.

. *Publish* - Click btn:[Publish]:
+
image::publish.gif["Publishing all items in the project", width=800]

. *Verify the published site* - Open {xp-url}/site/{cms-project-id}/master/hello-world[{xp-url}/site/{cms-project-id}/master/hello-world] in your browser. It shows the site as served from the `master` branch.
