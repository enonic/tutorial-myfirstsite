In this chapter, you'll publish the `Hello World` site, making it available outside Content Studio.

== Publishing

In Content Studio, you work in the `draft` branch. Publishing copies the selected items from `draft` to the `master` branch, which is the one visitors see.

=== Task: Publish the site

. *Select the site* - Select the `Hello World` site in the content list.

. *Open the Publishing Wizard* - Click the arrow next to the publish button in the top right corner, and select `Publish tree`. The Publishing Wizard opens, listing the site and everything below it.

. *Mark the content as ready* - Items that are `In progress` block publishing. If the wizard lists any, click btn:[Mark as ready].

. *Publish* - Click btn:[Publish now]:
+
image::publish.png["The Publishing Wizard listing the Hello World site and its child items", width=1440]

. *Verify the published site* - Open {xp-url}/site/{cms-project-id}/master/hello-world/norway[{xp-url}/site/{cms-project-id}/master/hello-world/norway] in a private browser window, where you are not logged in. It shows the page as served from the `master` branch:
+
image::result.png["The published page for Norway, with the description in the left column and the cities in the right column", width=1280]
