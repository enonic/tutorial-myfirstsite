In this chapter, you'll open Content Studio, explore the sample content your app imported, and create content of your own from the app's content types.

== Content Studio

Content Studio is the editorial interface of Enonic XP. The first time your app starts, it creates a content project called `{cms-project-displayname}` and imports sample content into it.

=== Task: Explore the sample content

. *Open Content Studio* - Open the XP menu on the right-hand side, and select `Content Studio`.
You should see some of the imported content:
+
image::imported-content.png["Tree structure showing countries and cities in Content Studio", width=1024]

. *Expand the content tree* - The root item of the tree is `Hello World`. Its icon shows that it is a site.
Expand it to reveal `Columbia`, `Norway`, `USA`, and `Templates` as child items.

TIP: Ignore the `Templates` folder for now. You'll get back to it in the <<page-templates#,page templates chapter>>.

== Sites and apps

A site is a content item with one or more apps assigned to it. Assigning an app gives the site, and Content Studio, access to what the app contains, such as content types and components.

=== Task: Find the app on the site

. *Open the site* - Select the `Hello World` content and click btn:[Edit].

. *Verify the assigned app* - In the form on the left-hand side, your app `{app-displayname}` is listed under `Applications`:
+
image::hello-world-content.png["The Hello World site content, with the My First Site app assigned to it", width=920]

== Content types

Every content item has a content type.

XP ships with a set of standard content types: `Shortcut`, `Folder`, `Site`, and a selection of media types that handle different kinds of files.

Apps can define their own content types. Your app contains two, `Country` and `City`, defined under `src/main/resources/site/content-types/`.
A content type is defined in a descriptor file. This is the descriptor of `City`:

.src/main/resources/site/content-types/city/city.xml
[source,xml]
----
include::{sourcedir}src/main/resources/site/content-types/city/city.xml[]
----

Content Studio turns the descriptor into an editing form:

image::city-form.png["Form for the city content type", width=920]

=== Task: Create your own content

. *Create a country* - Select the `Hello World` site, click btn:[New], and select the `Country` content type.
Fill in the form with a country of your choosing, and save it.

. *Add a city* - Select your new country, click btn:[New], and select the `City` content type.
Fill in the form, and save it. Add more cities if you like.

. *Verify the result* - Your country is listed in the content tree below `Hello World`, with its cities as child items.
