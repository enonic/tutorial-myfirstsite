In this chapter, you'll place two parts on a page: one that shows the details of a country, and one that lists its cities and can be configured by the editor.

== The country part

Parts are the visual building blocks of a page. Like the Text component, parts are added to regions, but you write their rendering yourself.

Your app has a part that shows details from the `Country` content type. Like a page component, it consists of a descriptor, a controller, and a view. Parts are placed in the `src/main/resources/site/parts/` folder:

.src/main/resources/site/parts/country/country.xml
[source,xml]
----
include::{sourcedir}/country/country.xml[]
----

.src/main/resources/site/parts/country/country.ts
[source,typescript]
----
include::{sourcedir}/country/country.ts[]
----

.src/main/resources/site/parts/country/country.html
[source,html]
----
include::{sourcedir}/country/country.html[]
----

=== Task: Add the country part

. *Open the country page* - In Content Studio, edit the country you added the region to in the <<regions#,previous chapter>>.

. *Insert the part* - Drag `Part` from the `Components` widget on the right-hand side into the region, and select `Country` in the list.

. *Verify the result* - The page should look something like this:
+
image::country-part-on-columbia.png["The country part on the Columbia page, showing facts about the country", width=600]

TIP: You can also manage the components on a page from the page component view. It shows the components as a tree, on the left-hand side or floating above the page editor.

image::page-component-input.png["The page component view, a tree structure for managing the components on a page", width=500]

== Configurable part

The `City list` part is slightly more advanced. It:

* lists the cities within a country
* shows an image of each city
* lets the editor choose the image cropping format

The part is also included in your app:

.src/main/resources/site/parts/city-list/city-list.xml
[source,xml]
----
include::{sourcedir}/city-list/city-list.xml[]
----

.src/main/resources/site/parts/city-list/city-list.ts
[source,typescript]
----
include::{sourcedir}/city-list/city-list.ts[]
----

.src/main/resources/site/parts/city-list/city-list.html
[source,html]
----
include::{sourcedir}/city-list/city-list.html[]
----

In the view, the `th:if` and `th:unless` attributes handle an empty list. If no cities are found, the template displays "No cities found". Otherwise, it loops through the cities and outputs the details of each one.

=== Task: Add and configure the city list

. *Insert the part* - Add a new part to the same region as before, and select `City list` in the list.

. *Configure the part* - With the part selected, change its settings in the panel on the right-hand side. For example, select `Widescreen` in the `Image crop` dropdown, and click btn:[Apply].

. *Verify the result* - The page lists the cities of the country, with images in the format you chose:
+
image::city-list-part-on-columbia.jpg["The city list part on the Columbia page, configured to show widescreen images", width=1227]

== Image service

Images are scaled and cropped in real time by the {xp-docs}/{xp-docs-version}/runtime/engines/site-engine/image-service[image service]. The part controller uses the `imageUrl()` function to create a link to each image, with the desired size and cropping.

== Logging

The `City list` controller also writes its model object to the log. Open a country page, then check the terminal where the sandbox is running to see what the controller passes to the view.
